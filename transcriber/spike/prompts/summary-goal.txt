<task>

<rules>
1. Figures and specifics are as valuable as advice: keep every one that was said, grouping figures about the same thing into one point ("Costs: rent $4.1M, staff $2.6M, software $0.3M"). Then, in order of value: advice said to {me} that applies before the goal is reached, written as a command; then what anyone asked for, promised or decided; then facts {me} can use. Leave out what does not serve the goal.
2. {me} already knows what {me} said: {me}'s own story goes only in the "{me} covered:" line, never in the points. {me}'s questions are not points; the answers to them are.
3. Leave out small talk: greetings, weekend plans, events, health and anything else that does not bear on the goal.
4. Each item appears once. If the same advice belongs in a section and in Next Steps, keep the section point short and the Next Step full.
5. A point may cite up to three moments of the call: a [mm:ss] and its copied words after the point for each moment it draws on.
6. Use names, never "you", "your", "I", "we", "Them" or "Me".
7. Next Steps: 2 to 5 actions {me} or someone else will take before the goal's next step (a meeting, an interview, a deadline), the most important first. Keep a day or date the way it was said ("next Friday").
8. Only what the transcript says or the goal states. No outside knowledge, no person who is not named in the transcript or the call's title. Keep (unclear) words as heard.
</rules>

<shape_rules>
Write the notes the way the example below is written. Measured on 10 notes the user likes:
1. About 11 words per bullet, never more than 20.
2. Each top bullet is one headline claim; put its figures, example or why in about one nested sub-bullet (two spaces, then "- ").
3. About a third of bullets open with a short label and a colon: "Real engine: franchise fees $2.78B".
4. Never start a bullet with a speaker's name or "Speaker N:". Name a person inside the sentence only when it matters.
5. 3 to 6 sections with plain topic headings, 3 to 5 headlines each; no section with a single bullet.
6. Next Steps: short commands with the owner in parentheses, and only for real actions; no "so that".
7. Length: about 300 to 450 words for a 1:1 or team meeting, 550 to 700 for a class or panel.
If work was split between people, add a section "Who's doing what" in the same style.
8. Every bullet, top and nested, still ends the way the shape does: its [mm:ss] and the <!-- 4 to 8 words copied exactly from that paragraph --> comment. The example leaves them out only to show the form; a bullet without them cannot be checked.
</shape_rules>
